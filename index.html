More than workwear

Built for the comeback.

2nd Chance Rebel Workwear comes from HardworkPNW LLC, a Washington construction company built on hands-on work and the belief that a person’s next chapter can be stronger than the last.

Apparel sales help fuel a bigger mission: create jobs, open doors into skilled trades, support housing stability and build real pathways to second chances. This is gear for people who know that grit is earned — and opportunity should be built.
